A tableware supplier built around the set

Most ceramic suppliers sell one shape well. QULABYZ exists because buyers kept asking for the opposite: a plate, a bowl and a mug that look like they were designed together, arrive together, and can be reordered together eighteen months later.

Who we are

Hong Kong office, Guangdong production

QULABYZ LIMITED is registered in Hong Kong and runs its sourcing and quality operation out of Chaozhou and Shantou in Guangdong, where the ceramic tableware cluster has been firing domestic ware for centuries. The Hong Kong entity handles contracts, export documents and payment; the Guangdong team handles moulds, decoration, sampling and inspection.

That split is deliberate. Buyers get an invoicing and documentation counterparty they can work with in English, on banking terms they recognise, while the people answering questions about crazing, rim chipping or glaze pooling are the ones standing on the factory floor.

We do not try to be everything in houseware. Ceramic tableware sets are the whole range, and the depth sits in decoration control and set matching rather than in catalogue width.

What we do

Four things, done properly

The service is narrow on purpose. Every capability below exists to make a multi-piece set land correctly at the other end.

1Set engineering

We start from the box, not from a single plate. Piece counts, rim profiles and stacking heights are worked out so the set packs efficiently and displays as a family.

  • Planogram-based piece counts
  • Shared rim and foot profiles
  • Stacking and carton cube optimisation

2Decoration control

Colour drift is the most common complaint on reorders. We keep the approved decal film and the colour standard on file, and pull them for every repeat run.

  • Under-glaze and on-glaze decal
  • Pad printing and embossed backstamp
  • Colour standard retained per buyer

3Compliance & testing

Food-contact testing is arranged per order through a third-party laboratory, with the report issued in the buyer's own name so it can be used for retail onboarding.

  • FDA 21 CFR 175.300
  • EU 1935/2004 and 84/500/EEC
  • LFGB, Prop 65 on request

4Export packing

Retail packing is prepared at source: barcodes, hang tags, multilingual labels, FBA cartons and pallet configurations, with a drop test before the run is released.

  • Colour, window and rigid gift boxes
  • Amazon / FBA carton preparation
  • Container loading photo report

Quality control

Three checks before a carton closes

Ceramics fail in predictable ways. The inspection plan is written against those failure modes rather than against a generic checklist.

In-line, at the kiln

Every piece is tapped and inspected as it leaves the kiln for crazing, warp and pinholes. Rejects are pulled at this stage, before decoration is applied, so a bad body never reaches the print line.

Decoration review

The first 50 decorated pieces of each run are compared against the approved sample under daylight lamps. Colour, registration and coverage must match before the rest of the batch is released.

Final AQL inspection

Finished cartons are sampled to AQL 2.5 major / 4.0 minor for chips, glaze defects, print alignment and packing integrity, with a drop test on the shipping carton and a photo report sent to the buyer.
