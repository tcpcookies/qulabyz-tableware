Before you write

What helps us quote faster

1

A reference image

Even a screenshot from a retail site is enough to identify the closest existing mould and tell you whether a new tool is needed.

2

A target landed price

Tell us what the set has to sell for and where. We will say honestly if the specification fits the price or has to be adjusted.

3

Quantity and timing

First order size and the date you need it on shelf decide the production slot. Earlier notice means a better shipping rate.
