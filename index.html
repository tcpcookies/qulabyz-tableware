Buyer questions

What importers usually ask

What is the minimum order quantity?
300 pieces per design for stock shapes and existing decorations. For a new mould or a fully custom decal the minimum is 500 pieces per shape, because the mould and the printing plate both have to be made. Mixed shapes can be combined inside one order to reach the quantity.
How long does a sample take, and what does it cost?
Stock-shape samples ship in 7–10 days. Custom decoration samples take 12–15 days because the decal has to be printed and fired. Sample charges are refunded against the bulk order once it is confirmed.
What is the production lead time?
25–35 days after sample approval and deposit for orders under 20,000 pieces. Orders above that, or orders that need a new mould, run 40–50 days. We confirm the date in writing on the proforma invoice rather than leaving it verbal.
Can we put our own logo or artwork on the pieces?
Yes. Options are under-glaze decal, on-glaze decal, pad printing and embossed backstamp. Under-glaze decoration is the most durable and is what we recommend for anything that will be machine washed. We will tell you if an artwork is too fine for the process before you commit.
Which food-safety standards can you test to?
FDA 21 CFR 175.300 for the United States, EU 1935/2004 with 84/500/EEC for Europe, plus LFGB for the German market and California Prop 65 where requested. Testing is done by a third-party laboratory and the report is issued in the buyer's name.
Is the tableware dishwasher and microwave safe?
All stoneware and porcelain bodies in our range are fired above 1,180 °C and are dishwasher safe. Microwave safety depends on the decoration: pieces with metallic or gold detailing are not microwave safe, and we mark this on the carton so retail staff can advise customers correctly.
What packing options are available?
Brown export carton, printed colour box, window box, rigid gift box, shrink-wrapped tray and pallet display. Inner dividers, barcodes, price tickets, multilingual labels and Amazon-ready FBA cartons can all be prepared. Cartons are drop-tested before the run is released.
What are the payment and shipping terms?
30% deposit with the balance against the bill of lading copy, by T/T. We quote FOB Shantou or FOB Shenzhen as standard, and can arrange CIF or DDP through our forwarder if you prefer a delivered price. L/C at sight is accepted for orders above USD 50,000.
